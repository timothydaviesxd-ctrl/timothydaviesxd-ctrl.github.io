Agentic experience patterns

Three per turn

A spoken answer never lists more than three things. If there are more, it offers them.

The problem

Lists that work on a screen collapse when they’re spoken. By the fourth item most listeners have lost the first, and they can’t scroll back to check.

The pattern

Cap spoken lists at three items, ordered by what matters most. If there are more, the agent says how many and offers to continue, or sends the full list to the screen.

Do

  • Lead with the most useful item
  • Say how many there are in total
  • Offer the rest, or show it

Avoid

  • Reading long lists because the data allows it
  • Ordering items by how the system stores them
  • Ending a list without saying whether there’s more

Example

  • PersonWhat’s on my list today?
  • AgentThree visits this morning: Market Street, the station and Hill Road. There are two more this afternoon. Want those too?

An illustrative example.

Where it came from

Developed on a voice agent for field sales reps at a FTSE 100 drinks bottler.
