Agentic experience patterns

Structure inside the conversation

When the agent needs precise details, it brings a small form into the conversation instead of asking question after question.

The problem

Conversation is good for intent and bad for precision. Collecting a date, a quantity and three options through back-and-forth chat is slow, error-prone and tiring, especially when the person already knows exactly what they want.

The pattern

Let the conversation establish what the person is trying to do, then present a compact, structured input inline, with sensible defaults already filled in. The person adjusts and confirms in one step, and the conversation carries on.

Do

  • Pre-fill everything the agent already knows
  • Keep the inline form to the few fields that matter
  • Return to conversation as soon as it’s submitted

Avoid

  • Ten questions in a row for ten fields
  • Sending people out to a separate page
  • Forms that aren’t accessible by keyboard and screen reader

Example

  • PersonI need to book an assessment for next week.
  • AgentHere are the details I’ve filled in. Check the date and location, then confirm.

Where it came from
