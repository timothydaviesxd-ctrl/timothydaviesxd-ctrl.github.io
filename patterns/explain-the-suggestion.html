Agentic experience patterns

Explain the suggestion

When an agent recommends something, it shows the evidence behind it.

The problem

Suggestions without reasons ask people to trust the system blindly. Experts ignore them, and everyone else either follows them too readily or not at all.

The pattern

Pair every suggestion with the evidence that produced it, placed where the person is already working. The reason should be short enough to scan and specific enough to check.

Do

  • Show the two or three signals that matter most
  • Put the explanation next to the suggestion, not behind a link
  • Let people dismiss a suggestion and say why

Avoid

  • Generic reasons such as “based on your data”
  • Explanations longer than the suggestion
  • Suggestions that can’t be traced back to anything

Example

  • AgentSuggested next step: book a follow-up review. Why: two missed appointments this month, and the last review was over a year ago.
