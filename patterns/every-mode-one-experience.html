Agentic experience patterns

Every mode, one experience

The same experience works through voice, text, touch and sign, so nobody is shut out by the mode they use.

The problem

Voice can remove barriers for someone who is blind or has limited mobility, and create them for someone who is Deaf, has a speech difference or is in a loud place. Choosing a single mode quietly decides who the agent is for.

The pattern

Design the experience first, then express it in every mode. Every task can be completed without voice, without a screen and without a mouse. Streaming answers tell screen-reader users when they’re finished, and every action the agent offers can be reached by keyboard.

Do

  • Test every task in each mode
  • Build with disabled people and Deaf communities, not just for them
  • Write accessibility requirements before the build

Avoid

  • Treating accessibility as a later phase
  • Voice-only or screen-only flows
  • Assuming AI will handle accessibility by default
