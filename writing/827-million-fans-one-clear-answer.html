827 million fans. One clear answer.

How the Formula 1 fan companion came together, experience first.

November 2026

827 million fans. Thousands of pages of new rules. One question: how do you make the 2026 FIA regulations make sense to a fan? That was the challenge behind the Agentforce fan companion on F1.com, and it’s the clearest example of how I work.

We didn’t start with the technology. We started with the fan: what they actually want to know, how much they want to hear, and what happens when the honest answer is “it’s complicated”.

I ran the discovery and experience workshops, then designed the conversation and response logic that turns dense regulation into answers people can follow. That included the parts most people never see: how the agent handles uncertainty, what it declines to answer, and how it stays on-brand under pressure. It went live eight weeks after the statement of work was signed.

What I took from it

Experience first: define what the fan needs, then use technology to close the gap. Refusals are design: what an agent won’t say matters as much as what it will, which is now written up as designed refusals and approved sources only. And answers aren’t the finish line. The next frontier is agents people come back to, not just agents that respond.

Timothy Davies is Global Director – Agentic Experience at Salesforce. More in the patterns library.
