Formula 1

The Agentforce fan companion on F1.com

Live eight weeks after the statement of work was signed, for 827 million fans.

The problem
The 2026 regulations were the biggest change to the technical rules in the sport’s history, spread across thousands of pages. Fans needed answers they could follow, from an agent F1 could trust in public.
My decision
Start with the fan’s question, not the feature list.

Thousands of pages of regulation, resolved into one clear answer.

What I designed
The discovery and experience workshops, then the conversation and response logic: answers only from approved sources, depth adapted to each fan, and a plain answer when a question is out of scope.
What changed
The agent went live on F1.com eight weeks after the statement of work was signed, and the method became a team asset.

What I’d do differently. Get involved before the proposal is written, and design for return visits as well as answers.

A FTSE 100 drinks bottler

Voice for people on the move

The objective and use cases defined in a single day. The build follows the day I mapped.

The problem
Field sales reps spend their day between shop visits and behind the wheel. A voice agent had to help without asking for their eyes.
My decision
Design for the rep’s day, not the device.

Only what’s vital earns a place in speech.

What I designed
An information hierarchy and voice patterns across in-car, earbuds and in-app chat. Pricing goes to the screen, orders are read back, and never more than three items in a turn.
What changed
The build follows the shape of the day I mapped: prepare, visit, close out.

What I’d do differently. Test data and organisational readiness in the first week, so the design has somewhere to land.

Volvo Group, at PolSource

Configure, price and sell Volvo Trucks on Salesforce

A working configure-and-price prototype, and a Conga Innovation Award.

The problem
Selling a truck is a complex journey of configuration and pricing, and the sales process needed to reflect how trucks are actually built and sold.
My decision
Redesign the sales process around the way trucks are configured, priced and sold.

Configuration and pricing, designed as one journey.

What I designed
An off-site Salesforce Innovation Center session with the customer, then a working prototype that configures a truck and prices it through the CPQ quotation process.
What changed
The work won a Conga Innovation Award.

How I work

The same five moves, whether it’s an agent for millions of fans or a tool for a field team.

  1. Start with the person

    Every engagement begins with the people who’ll use it, and the moment they’re in.

  2. Name the problem

    I take a blank page to a clear brief that leaders and teams can agree on.

  3. Prototype in days

    I build working prototypes myself in Claude, Cursor and Figma, so leaders decide on something real.

  4. Design what it won’t do

    Refusals, read-backs and limits are part of the experience, not an afterthought.

  5. Build it for everyone

    Voice, text, touch and sign. An agent that excludes people isn’t finished.
