Accessibility statement

I design for accessibility for a living, so this site should meet the standard I ask of others.

Last reviewed November 2026

What I aim for

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. It hasn’t been independently audited yet.

What you can do here

Use the Accessibility button at the top of any page to reduce motion, switch to high contrast, increase text size, add readable spacing, underline links, or turn on a reading guide. The site follows your device’s reduce-motion setting automatically. Every control works with a keyboard, and colour contrast meets AA throughout.

Known limitations

The voice demo on the home page uses your browser’s built-in speech, so the voice varies by browser and device. Captions run either way. The animated film and the particle background on the home page are decorative. Both stop when motion is reduced, and the film never plays until you press play. Settings in the accessibility panel are not yet remembered between visits.

Tell me about a problem

If something doesn’t work for you, please email me with the page and what happened. I’ll reply and fix it.
